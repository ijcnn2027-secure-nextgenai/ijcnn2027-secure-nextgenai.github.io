Subject: Call for Papers | IJCNN 2027 Special Session on Securing Next-Generation AI | Deadline: January 31, 2027

Dear Colleagues,

We are pleased to invite you to submit original research contributions to the following Special Session at IJCNN 2027:

SECURING THE NEXT GENERATION OF AI: AGENTIC, COLLABORATIVE AND MULTIMODAL INTELLIGENCE

International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN 2027)
June 14–18, 2027 | Cape Town, South Africa

Paper submission deadline: January 31, 2027

SCOPE AND MOTIVATION

As AI evolves beyond isolated predictive models toward autonomous agents, conversational systems, collaborative learning architectures, and multimodal intelligence, new attack surfaces and trust dependencies emerge.

This Special Session aims to bring together researchers and practitioners working on the security, robustness, and trustworthiness of next-generation AI systems, as well as their innovative applications to cybersecurity. Theoretical, methodological, and experimental contributions are welcome, including novel threat models, neural architectures, benchmarks, and reproducible evaluations.

TOPICS OF INTEREST

- Security and adversarial robustness of neural and agentic AI systems.
- Trustworthy coordination, communication, and decision-making in multi-agent systems.
- Trustworthy conversational agents: user guidance, usability, transparency, safety, factual reliability, privacy, robustness, and resistance to adversarial attacks and manipulation across multi-turn interactions.
- Secure, robust, and privacy-preserving cooperative and collaborative learning, including federated and decentralized settings.
- Adversarial attacks and defenses in foundation models and LLM-based systems.
- Security and robustness of computer vision, multimodal AI, and vision-language models.
- Detection of deepfakes, synthetic media, and manipulated content.
- AI-driven cyber threat detection, intelligence, and response.
- Collaborative and multi-agent approaches to cybersecurity.
- Knowledge-guided and neuro-symbolic methods for trustworthy AI.
- Explainability, transparency, and auditability of neural and autonomous systems.
- Provenance, traceability, and verification of AI decisions and actions.
- Security assessment, adversarial benchmarking, and red teaming of AI systems.

SUBMISSION AND PUBLICATION

Papers must be submitted through Microsoft CMT as regular papers (Main Track), selecting “Securing the Next Generation of AI: Agentic, Collaborative and Multimodal Intelligence” as the primary Subject Area.

All submissions will undergo the same peer-review process as regular conference papers. Accepted contributions will be included in the conference proceedings.
