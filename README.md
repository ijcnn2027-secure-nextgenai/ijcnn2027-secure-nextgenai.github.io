# Secure NextGen AI @ IJCNN 2027

## Securing the Next Generation of AI: Agentic, Collaborative and Multimodal Intelligence

Special Session at the 2027 International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN 2027).

Cape Town, South Africa | June 14–18, 2027

## About

This Special Session brings together researchers and practitioners working on the security, robustness, reliability, and trustworthiness of agentic, conversational, collaborative, and multimodal AI systems.
